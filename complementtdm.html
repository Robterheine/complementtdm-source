How to read these results
  • Band (5–95%): 90% plausible range for the true concentration. It does not include assay error, so a future measured value may fall outside it.
  • P(trough > target): chance the true trough stays above your target, at your chosen dates.
  • Median trough [5–95%]: the same distribution in µg/mL.
